A church that feels like home

New Wave of Glory Parish is a parish of The Redeemed Christian Church of God. We exist to make heaven, to take as many people with us as possible, and to have a good time doing it.

Whether you are new to faith or have walked with God for years, there is a seat for you here, and people who will help you grow.

Our vision: raising people who carry the glory of God into their homes, workplaces and nation.
